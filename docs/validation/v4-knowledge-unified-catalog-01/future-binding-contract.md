# Future Evidence Binding contract — not implemented

Input: `Knowledge Catalog + Actual Calculated Evidence + Actual MBTI Source + Product Context`.
Output: eligible candidates and explicit withheld candidates. Types are in `knowledge-integration-foundation/types.ts`; the compiler never accepts a real person and never produces eligible interpretations.

Source/calculation/semantic definitions, reference views, audit metadata, binding designs, rejected material and fixtures are not customer interpretations. Editorial material requires an approved claim and cannot supply a standalone fact. Only explicitly reviewed interpretation/Fusion/product/relationship candidates may enter binding.

Twelve hard gates precede all ranking, in this order:

1. Source locator, version and applicable hash scheme match.
2. The interpretation's meaning review permits the proposed use.
3. The existing producer actually supports the required fact and scope.
4. The producer emitted the exact required calculated Evidence; a catalog path is not evidence.
5. Every root belongs to the actual person. Individual Fusion must keep the same person's two roots.
6. The actual subject/recipient direction matches, with independently calculated recipient-tenGod evidence. AB cannot be reversed into BA.
7. Every ancestor satisfies the domain policy; neither the top node nor `some(allowed)` suffices.
8. All applicable confirmed/conditional intervals intersect; excluded and unknown windows remain explicit.
9. User state is supplied and satisfies the condition. Missing MBTI/hour/context cannot be supplied from research.
10. Counterexamples, prohibitions and safety boundaries permit the proposed application.
11. The actual product and relationship question are supported by reviewed specialist material.
12. Customer use has explicit approval. Every catalog entry in this delivery has approval **false**.

Failure returns candidate ID, failed gate, related source IDs, required Evidence, found Evidence and necessary follow-up action. No silent filtering; no numeric quality score can repair a failed gate. Ranking output must carry the complete gate receipt and approval record.

`sourceGroups`: existing manager/team and business-partner contracts declare `allSourcesWithinGroupRequired: true`; `compatible_actually_displayed_group_only` restricts recall to actually introduced compatible groups. This is AND within selected compatible groups, not permission to treat the entire list as an arbitrary OR. `all_direct_sources` keeps its original stronger contract. Any other undocumented grouping is `UNKNOWN_GROUP_SEMANTICS` and must be withheld. Empty arrays do not create facts.

Definitions and actual facts remain separate. A canonical pair definition requires a real supported canonical-pair Evidence instance before use. A–B labels require actual owner IDs. Parent/child and manager/team roles are asymmetric. Business equity, contract, authority, assets, debts and revenue remain UNKNOWN without inputs. MBTI pair notes do not prove a relationship outcome or authorize transferring romantic scoring domains.

Temporal constraints preserve 10-year Dayun cycles versus the 14-year product window, selected annual year, and actual Jie/Dayun monthly segments. The future Evidence Graph supplies times and certainty; this compiler calculates none. Unknown scopes are not promoted to timeless traits.

Research review, reference integrity, traditional-source review, MBTI direct/inferred coverage, meaning review, calculation applicability, product applicability and customer approval are independent. Direct MBTI prose is not empirical verification of a person. Reading a traditional source does not validate prediction accuracy.
